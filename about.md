## About
This mod adds a **draggable menu with action buttons inside the level UI** to work with your gameplay more quickly!

---

### Menu
A <cg>menu</c> will appear in-game, it'll have your essentials such as practice mode and restart level. Since the intention is for the player to do stuff quicker, <cr>these buttons will have no confirmation pop-up and will immediately function as intended</c>.

<cy>Drag</c> the <cg>top-left button</c> to position the menu wherever on the screen you would like it to be. You may also <cy>press</c> said button without moving it to toggle the menu when you need it out of the way.

> ![✳](frame:collaborationIcon_001.png) <cg>*You can choose which buttons you actually want on this menu via the mod's settings.*</c>
